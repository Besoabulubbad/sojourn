# feature:bookings

Implements the design-system card **ScreenBookings** (with its states from **ScreenStates**): upcoming and past stays, the upcoming card leading with what the guest needs on arrival day.

- **Tab:** "Bookings", then Upcoming or Past (`SojournSegmentedControl`). Upcoming: each stay as a `SojournBookingCard` with its countdown ("Check-in in 12 days", "Check-in tomorrow", "Staying now"), "Directions" (the platform maps app at the stay's area) and "Check-in guide"; the first has its terms under it: free cancellation and what happens after it, the deposit and when it is released, check-in. Past: completed and cancelled stays, as a record. Empty: what will appear, and "Explore stays" on Upcoming.
- **Check-in guide** (sheet, half height, drag for full): check-in time and method, check-out time, the booked address, the key code and the Wi‑Fi. The code and the Wi‑Fi (network and password) show from arrival day until check-out, never before (ScreenBookings card), left to right, with "Copy" and "Copy password" (the platform clipboard, then "Key code copied" / "Password copied"). Before then the sheet says when they appear; a host check-in says the host meets you; a booking made before key codes existed gets its code by message.
- **Booking page** (`BookingDetailsKey`, from the tab, Explore's next stay or the confirmation's "View booking"; not designed on the card, built from its parts): the booking card; while the stay is to come, the booked address (flat and building, then area and city) with "Copy address" and "Add to calendar" (an all-day entry from check-in through check-out through the system's calendar editor, `CalendarSaver`); the arrival reminder card while the stay is upcoming (`SojournReminderCard`, ADR 0019; cancelling the booking cancels it); the total paid; the terms while the stay is to come; and "Cancel booking" before check-in. The cancel sheet says what comes back under the stay's policy: everything until the free-cancellation deadline; after it, all but the first 7 nights (pro rata of the total), or nothing for a stay of 7 nights or fewer. "Cancel booking" is the danger button; "Keep booking" closes the sheet. Offline, the sheet stays open and says so. A cancelled booking says so and frees its nights.
- **Explore:** from two days before arrival until check-out, the stay leads Explore (`ObserveArrivingStay`); otherwise Explore resumes the last search.
- **States:** bookings are on the device, so both screens work offline; cancelling needs the network.

**Navigation keys** (declared in `core:navigation`): `BookingsKey` (the tab's root), `BookingDetailsKey(bookingId)`.
