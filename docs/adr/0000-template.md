# NNNN — Short title in the imperative or as a noun phrase

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | deprecated | superseded by NNNN (link the superseding ADR)

## Context and problem statement

Two to five sentences. What forces are at play, what has to be decided, and why now. Link the design-system card, finding, issue or standard that raised it.

- A quality or constraint that matters here (e.g. "must work identically on iOS", "must be enforceable by a tool")
- Another driver
